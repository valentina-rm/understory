UnderStory

Vital ideas and courageous voices are taking root below the mainstream.

A space where scientists, artists, professors, financiers, and craftspeople of diverse generations, cultures, and regions reflect on the same questions about our bond with the earth, with each other, and with ourselves.

Read the index → What this is
The premise
Vital ideas grow below what gets attention, long before they reach the canopy.

In nature, the understory is the first layer of the forest. It's where plant life grows in shaded regions, beneath the canopy. The most vital movements begin here, in the nutrient-rich margins.

In society, that translates into the ideas, voices, and practices that emerge outside dominant institutions, headlines, and elite consensus. UnderStory is where those ideas gather force before they go mainstream.

The project

A living archive of constructive ideas.

01

Voices across disciplines, and lived experience

Invitation-only contributors who are diverse in trade, age, geography and lived experience. Not chosen for prestige, but for their wisdom, clarity, and constructive imagination.

02

Standard prompts, comparable responses

Contributors answer from a same small set of questions and reflection types, making patterns visible across very different voices.

03

Short, honest, readable contributions

2,500 characters (~400 words) or less. Prose or poetry. Simple and accessible language.

04

A space where unlike minds think together

A musician, a professor, a film-maker, a scientist, an investor, a carpenter, and a novelist from multiple regions, and across generations might all answer the same question revealing something about the future we can envision.

Types of reflection

No serious crisis should go to waste.

Tell it as it is

Misconception

"People often think X, but, actually...": A hot-take: an alternative to a widely held view that may be mainstream but misses something essential.

Offer policy advice

Recommendation

"We should/could/shall do X...": A concrete policy, institutional, or cultural change that would unlock meaningful progress.

Dare to dream

Radical vision

"What if we X ...": A vivid description of the world we want — and the shift that could make it possible.

The prompts

Similar questions, so voices can be compared.

Climate & nature

Misconception

What do most people get radically wrong about our relationship with the natural world?

Recommendation

What policy, institutional, or cultural change would help societies live within the Earth's limits?

Radical vision

Describe a morning in 2050 in a society that has made its peace with nature. What changed?

Power & the commons

Misconception

What truth about power, inequality, race, or gender could help us heal our bond with each other?

Recommendation

What policy, institutional, or cultural change that would foster inclusive systems that strengthen social bonds?

Radical vision

What does shared prosperity actually look like in someone's daily life?

Meaning & connection

Misconception

What are we getting wrong about what makes life meaningful, and who benefits from that confusion?

Recommendation

What policy, institutional, design or cultural change would help people thrive?

Radical vision

It's 2050. Something has shifted in how humans relate to each other and to themselves. What changed?
